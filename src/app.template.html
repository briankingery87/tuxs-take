Tux’s Take is the post-game half of the CFB Atlas, a college football data and mapping project covering all 666 NCAA programs across FBS, FCS, Division II and Division III. Built and maintained by Brian Kingery. One HTML file, no server, no API key - it reads the published services live on every load.
Under the Couch · The CFB Atlas Experience · Ask the Atlas
Data
CFB Atlas hosted feature services, ArcGIS Online.
Source data: College Football Data (CFBD) results, box scores and betting lines. The Aftermath Index and the Tux grade are computed by the weekly notebook, not published by anyone else.
Recaps are machine-written and labeled per row; the numbers in them are not.
Built with
Leaflet 1.9.4 · Esri keyless dark canvas basemap.
Team marks are ESPN CDN assets referenced by the Teams layer.
TUX is a fictional character. School marks are used for identification only. Gamble responsibly. 18+.
No API keys, no tracking, no server.
